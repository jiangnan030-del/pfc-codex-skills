# Formulas

## Hoek-Brown style fitted relation in the chapter

The chapter retains a nonlinear fitted relation in the principal-stress plane:

$$
\sigma_{1}=\sigma_{3}+161\times\left(18.7\frac{\sigma_{3}}{161}+0.8725\right)^{0.4045}
$$

Use it as a chapter-specific fitted example rather than a universal law.

where:

- $\sigma_1$: major principal stress
- $\sigma_3$: confining stress / minor principal stress
- the constants come from the chapter's equivalent-crystal calibration and fit

## Compression-tension ratio interpretation

The chapter reports the compression-tension contrast through:

$$
\text{UCS/TS ratio} = \frac{161\ \text{MPa}}{7.5\ \text{MPa}} \approx 21.47
$$

This ratio is used as an interpretation metric showing that the equivalent crystal model better matches brittle-rock behavior than a standard BPM with an artificially high tensile strength.

## Reporting rule

When reusing the fitted formula:

- say that it is a chapter-specific calibration result
- do not present it as a universally transferable parameter set
- pair it with the validation context: tension, compression, crack pattern, and envelope shape
